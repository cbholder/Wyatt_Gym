The rotation
Why full body Three sessions, every other day, each one hits legs, chest, back, shoulders and arms. Everything gets trained three times a week instead of once, and that frequency is what actually drives strength up at your age.

ALPHA is heavy. BRAVO is volume with different lifts so nothing gets beat up. CHARLIE is lighter and faster.
Only got 20 minutes? Do the first five. Every day is built so the top five cover your whole body. Anything past five is bonus, not the point.
How to add weight Hit the top of the rep range on every set and the app tells you to go up. Small jumps — 5 lbs on machines and cables, 2.5 per side on a bar. Small jumps you keep beat big jumps you lose.

Form first, always. If the last two reps get ugly, the weight is too heavy. And get a spotter before you load the bench up.
